Review date: 2026-09-06. Baseline: `e919c3f94b0a6a7beeda6f84706932ef0e82535c` (Phase 6A).

**Decision: there is insufficient evidence to create a defensible `hs2_candidate` magnetic-cycle timing profile.** No flight-candidate timing profile is created or activated. The companion [machine-readable evidence register](../basilisk_runner/config/magnetic_control_evidence.json) is documentation, is marked `runtime_usable: false`, and is not a runtime configuration.

## Engineering disposition

- **Actuators:** three actuators are consistently described. I1's two CR0002 rods plus MT01A air coil is the strongest model-specific candidate; M1's three in-house cells remains an unresolved selection conflict. Installed axes, polarity, winding/driver revisions and transfer curves are not released by this evidence.
- **Electrical consistency:** the MT01A/older MT01 dipoles and voltage limits are not interchangeable. I1 itself gives 13.2 W maximum for the air coil but 12 W peak for all ADCS, while its thermal table and B1 use 1.75 W for the coil. Peak/average, supply/winding and duty definitions must be reconciled.
- **Rates:** I1's 10 Hz managers/application are stronger specific interface evidence than the 5 Hz MDD/explicitly outdated software proposal, but no approved disposition or measured schedule establishes a winner. VN-100's 200 Hz magnetometer capability is not an intended control, packet or whole-cycle rate.
- **Sampling:** B3 explicitly intends raw VN-100 magnetic data. The I1 manager payload names angular rate, acceleration and timestamp, while BDot requires current/previous B; the producer and magnetic acquisition/freshness contract remain unclosed in those tables.
- **Drive interface:** I1's PC104 table and comment[i] support three PWM plus three GPIO; its software tables still specify six PWM. I5 corroborates ADCS GPIO/PWM connector signals. Their actual polarity/enable/brake truth table is not established.
- **Feedback:** I5 documents INA3221 rail monitoring at 1 Hz. This does not establish per-coil current feedback. I1's signed applied-duty telemetry describes commands, not measured winding current or dipole.
- **Quiet timing:** burst-then-zero-current sampling is qualitative. `RESET_WAIT_TICKS` is explicitly a device-reset wait, not post-actuation magnetic settling. Required quiet time, current decay, field settling, aperture and full command-window timing remain unsupported.
- **Contamination/test evidence:** the 15.24 mm figure is stack pitch, explicitly separate from the missing MTA–IMMU separation. No qualifying completed magnetic/current/settling/HIL measurement was located in the bounded reviewed evidence. T1 records incomplete ADCS tests; T2/T3 contain unfilled measurement/results sections; B3's verification prose supplies no traceable measured records.

A useful consistency inference, **not a proposed operating point**, is that H1's nominal 5 V / 51 ohm and 2.3 A m^2/A give approximately 0.22549 A m^2, rather than its nominal 0.2 A m^2. Likewise, treating I1's 12 V boost as continuous voltage across the regression 4.4 ohm winding would imply approximately 2.73 A and 32.73 W. That hypothetical calculation is not evidence that the winding actually receives 12 V DC; it illustrates why rail voltage cannot substitute for a characterized PWM/current-limited drive chain.

## Minimum inputs required before a flight-candidate cycle

1. **ADCS / Structures / configuration control:** Released installed BOM/part revisions, PCB/schematic and coil/sensor-to-body positions, matrices and positive-current polarity; disposition of COTS versus in-house and MT01A versus MT01.
2. **ADCS / EPS / CDH / Thermal:** Driver part/mode and pin truth table, actual rails/winding voltage, current limiting or feedback, configured carrier/burst parameters; measured per-axis m(I,T), current/power, saturation and safe thermal/duty envelope.
3. **ADCS / CDH:** VN-100 variant/interface/register packet and filter/output settings, internal magnetic acquisition epoch/aperture, raw data producer and delivery latency/freshness policy; external sensor inventory if any.
4. **ADCS / magnetic test lead:** Approved magnetic-error/cleanliness threshold plus synchronized current and field-versus-time after representative/worst-case bursts at installed geometry; measured valid quiet/settling window including filter recovery.
5. **CDH / ADCS integration:** Approved 5/10 Hz rate-group/control/cycle mapping; trace of acquisition, compute, command latch and physical current on/off under flight firmware, including clock offset, worst-case queue latency and jitter.

Each measurement package needs hardware/firmware/configuration revisions, operating conditions, calibrated instruments, synchronized raw traces, uncertainty, acceptance criteria and reviewer disposition. A procedure, procurement record or assertion of HIL does not close these inputs.

The direct timing decision needs both a valid-acquisition criterion and measured recovery to that criterion, followed by an approved mapping from acquisition through computation and drive to the next burst. Electrical/thermal and configuration inputs establish whether that schedule is admissible on the actual assembly.

## Recommended sensitivity studies while evidence is pending

No sweep is executed or new numerical range invented in Phase 6B. These are future ASSUMED studies, not hardware-qualified settings.

| Quantity | Evidence-based anchors or study target | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Manager/control cadence | 5 and 10 Hz are competing documented design assumptions. | First distinguish rate-group tick from raw acquisition and full burst-cycle period. Current distinct-phase 0.1 s-grid implementation has minimum whole cycle 0.4 s. |
| Burst duty and period | Historical 80% PDR and 50/20/10% mode-budget values can be labeled sensitivity anchors; Phase 6A 40% remains TEST-ONLY. | Vary feasible burst/period pairs; separate carrier PWM duty. No proposed setting is declared thermally/electrically flight-admissible. |
| Sample age and actuation latency | Sample-to-compute and compute-to-actuation delay/jitter versus burst/period; explicit synthetic assumptions only. | No measured numerical range available; do not silently adopt diagnostic 0.1 s delays or serial allocation as measured delays. |
| Quiet/settling and acquisition phase | Software gate and acquisition-age sensitivity; hardware-valid range pending current/field/filter measurements. | The present ideal sensor cannot predict contamination validity. Do not invent a physical decay constant, aperture or cleanliness magnitude. |
| Delivered moment and electrical/power envelope | Separate conditional CR0002+MT01A, older MT01 and in-house evidence branches; vary effective duty and calibrated gain/limits once bounded. | Never mix mismatched voltage/dipole/resistance endpoints into a claimed real actuator; keep uncalibrated sensitivity explicitly ASSUMED. |
| Alignment uncertainty | Separate documented 3 degree knowledge bounds and tighter budget allocations as labeled scenarios. | No measured mounting/error distribution or known body mapping; sweeps do not certify actual geometry. |

The current architecture requires four distinct nonzero durations on the 0.1 s plant grid, giving a minimum complete cycle of 0.4 s even with zero additional settling. It therefore cannot represent an entire 5 or 10 Hz magnetic cycle without a separately authorized timing-architecture change. A 5/10 Hz manager may instead govern a longer burst cycle; the evidence does not settle that mapping. The present ideal sensor also cannot determine how much physical contamination a shorter quiet window would admit.

## Dominant uncertainties

| Priority | Uncertainty | Engineering effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delivered torque and usable actuation duty | Unknown actual air-coil/driver dipole, gain, current and thermal limits directly change available magnetic torque and average control authority. |
| 2 | Magnetic sample validity | Unmeasured local residual field/remanence, coil decay and sensor filtering can corrupt sampled B even when the simulation has zero commanded dipole. |
| 3 | Sample age, cadence and burst phase | Long/variable delay and held commands change the relationship of measured B/rate to actuation; 5/10 Hz manager labels alone do not constrain this. |
| 4 | Axes and polarity | Incorrect coil/sensor transforms or sign can redirect torque and invalidate the controller's field/command relationship. |

These are engineering priorities, not quantified sensitivity rankings from a new run. Stronger actuation can also increase contamination or required off-time; commanded dipole, delivered dipole and usable sampling duty must be assessed together.
