## Frame and alignment contract

N is Earth-centered ICRF/J2000; B is the mathematical simulation body. sigma_BN represents B relative to N, C_BN maps N components to B. For a sensor S, C_SB maps B to S, so b_S=C_SB*C_BN*b_N and b_B=C_SB^T*b_S for a proper rotation. The existing identity C_SB is explicitly ASSUMED and enforced by config validation.

Physical B axes/origin and installed sensor/optical axes are not established. Per-sensor origin and polarity, housing datum, right-handed axis definitions, pixel axes and optical boresights must be released before applying any mounting transform.

## Observability and mission conditions

Independent engineering inference; not released HS-2 mode logic. For one known inertial vector b_N with measurement b_S=C_SB*C_BN*b_N, the attitude Jacobian is proportional to the vector cross-product matrix, rank two for a nonzero vector: rotation about that vector is instantaneously unobservable. Two correctly timed/calibrated non-collinear vector directions can locally constrain attitude; conditioning degrades near collinearity. A photodiode intensity is not by itself a full direction. Gyros propagate an initialized attitude but do not supply an absolute orientation or guarantee bias observability. Temporal field variation can improve observability under an appropriate dynamic model/excitation; it is not an instantaneous guarantee. Accelerometers in free fall cannot be treated as a terrestrial gravity-direction aid.

No new mode logic is designed here. Mode names and measurement intent come from [I1](#source-i1) 4.1.4, 4.1.8; ICD-ADCS-001; cover 2026-02-06; internal rev 5 dated 2026-09-01; [OP1](#source-op1) ADCS operations unfilled, as recorded in D1/D2; MOP-001 cover 2026-08-20; history through 2026-08-28; [B3](#source-b3) 2.4, 2.5, 2.8, 4.1; No release ID/date/approval established; modified 2026-09-03.

**Which terms can currently be quantified?** Only vendor component specifications, documented requirement/allocation candidates, and conditional sensitivities. None of the six terms has an independently validated installed HS-2 performance value.

**Which terms are completely blocked as flight predictions?** Estimator residual and bias drift, installed sensor/optical alignment, control tracking, real timing error and the combined total all lack required evidence; no flight error distribution is selected.

**Which uncertainties are likely to dominate?** Potentially consequential: raw-sensor versus tracker-feedback policy; gyro bias/drift over unaided intervals; contaminated magnetic data; coarse Sun response/coverage; optical alignment and sample-age errors. This is engineering risk ranking, not a numerical variance ranking. Existing budget assertion that control dominates is not independently established.

**Enough evidence for attitude KNOWLEDGE accuracy?** No. No current complete estimator, approved metric, installed error model or independent synchronized validation.

**Enough evidence for CONTROL pointing accuracy?** No. Estimator, hardware/magnetic validity, geometry and magnetic pointing/hold validation remain open.

**Enough evidence for TOTAL payload pointing accuracy?** No. Terms, frames, timing, confidence, correlations and inclusion boundaries are not closed; do not RSS unmatched assumptions.

Keep estimator knowledge, sensor-to-body alignment, control/tracking, payload-to-body boresight, timing/sample age and total payload pointing separate. Record which alignment/thermal/truth terms are already inside each residual. Do not combine mismatched confidence levels, bounded biases and correlated residuals into a pointing number.
